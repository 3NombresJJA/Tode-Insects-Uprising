The Uprising of the Insects

Tode was a happy frog who ruled over his peaceful garden, devouring insects to his heart's content to satisfy his insatiable appetite. However, fed up with being the gluttonous tyrant's daily feast, the bug community united in a fierce retaliation. Armed to the stingers and determined to reclaim the lawn, the swarm launched a massive uprising to overthrow their long-time predator. Now, amidst the chaos, Tode must leap into battle, hone his tongue, and crush the rebellion to take back absolute control of his home.
